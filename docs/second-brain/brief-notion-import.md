# Brief — bringing your Notion in

Written 19 October 2026 after reading the public index and two of its databases.
Nothing imported yet.

## What is there

About 20 databases, all set up by **Instinct** (another AI assistant) under Life Tasks:

| Group | Databases | Feeds questions |
|---|---|---|
| Goals and progress | Progress log, Daily hours + tasks (vs 60h/week), Work hours, Daily checklist, Tasks | 6, 7, 12–16 — what you do, reliably do and skip |
| People and outreach | Outreach list, Berkeley alum engineers, Inbound tracker, Investors, conversation targets | 22–26 — who is in your life, who you answer to |
| Content and reading | Saved Links (with a reason for each), TikTok scripts and ideas, research papers, proof-of-need | 18–21 — what you pay attention to and are learning; the reference bank (goal 23) |

## The catch: three kinds of text, and only one is yours

Seen in *Daily hours + tasks* and *Saved Links*:

1. **Instinct's suggestions** — e.g. "Suggested: write audience, promise and format in one line…". Written by the assistant, not you. **Never a claim about you.** At most: "this was suggested to you on 12 October".
2. **Logged numbers and ticks** — hours worked, "content done", dates, statuses. Not your prose, but a record of what you did, like a calendar. **Used as behaviour, not as your words.**
3. **What you wrote** — reviews, notes, your own task names, the messages Instinct filed links from. **Your own words.**

*Saved Links*' "Purpose" column is Instinct's wording of why you sent each link. Kept as the link's reason in the reference bank, marked as inferred.

## How it would go in

- You export once: Notion → **Settings → Export → Markdown & CSV**, subpages included. A zip in the project folder.
- Each database maps column by column to one of the three kinds above. I propose the mapping from the export and you skim it before anything is saved — it decides what the product believes about you.
- Rows land as items with the right authorship, so the protections already built (no "you" claims from text that isn't yours) apply automatically.
- Then the question store's researcher reads the new material and the answers move — the daily-brief test gets the daily data it was missing.

**Cost:** reading it in, roughly $1–3 depending on size; confirmed before spending.
**Later:** a live Notion connector so it stays current (goal 1). The export proves the value first.
