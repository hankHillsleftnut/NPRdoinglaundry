# Comms notes for after the iOS build (v2)

Things to revisit once the app is built. They aren't for now, and none is decided.

## "Not a journal that talks back"
- **Founder, 20 Oct (comment c-1bkx3d on the paywall, decision 06):** "this is not a journal that talks back, this is a full fledged self actualization machine. I like this phrasing, but note that it could be a comms risk."
- **The phrasing:** the paywall benefit row currently reads "a journal that talks back · talk or type; retro asks the next question". The founder's own framing of the product is bigger: a self-actualisation machine.
- **Why it's a comms risk:**
  - "Self-actualisation" makes a strong promise about personal change. That invites scepticism and App Store review scrutiny of wellbeing or therapeutic claims.
  - It can read as grand or cultish in ads and press.
  - It raises the bar the first week of use has to clear.
- **What to do later:**
  - Test the framing with real users after v2. Compare "a journal that talks back" with "self-actualisation" against something in between ("it helps you become who you said you'd be").
  - Check App Store guideline 1.4.1 (health and medical claims).
  - Decide where each line belongs (store page, paywall, onboarding, press). The big claim may fit the founder story and press better than the UI.
