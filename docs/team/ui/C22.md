### C22 · Mini-player (floating)                                     size: M
Place in the tree: journal › JR-1 › mini-player (every tab while something is loaded; paywall variant later with P07)

Needs: UI3.0, #42 (`RArtwork`), C18 (sits above it), T01        Unblocks: P10, P13, P07 (variant)

Why: whatever's playing stays in reach on every tab, small and quiet, one tap from the full player.

Inputs:
- Spec: `design-system/components/mini-player.md` (its 286×42 paywall variant is superseded); reference §C22; findings c-4d5kcg (A: 2pt line), c-5hyjn9 (paywall uses the 64)
- Paper: part 45 `GT1-0` (states `GWY-0`, in the app `GXK-0`); paywall instance `c:mini-player/paused` `22TR-0` (ON-12)
- Code anchors: `RArtwork`, `RProgressHairline`, `RSpinner`, `RMotion.sheet`

Spec:
- **Size:** width = screen − 2 × margin (354 on 402; SE screen − 40), height 64 (not `size.miniPlayer` 42), `radius.control` pill (`RShape.pill`), floating 12pt above the tab bar.
- **Layers, bottom to top:**
  1. floating-tier shadows (ambient `0 10 30 −10` warm 30%, cast `0 4 10 −4`, contact `0 1 1.5`)
  2. body `background.raised` 92% over `material.bar`, a 0.5 warm outer hairline, a 1pt white inner stroke brighter on top
  3. 44 artwork, radius 22 (`RArtwork` per kind, grain)
  4. title 14 semibold, one line, ellipsis; subline Geist 12 `text.label` ("today's brief · part 2 · 1 min left"); insets 10, the title column flexes
  5. a 44 wash play well
  6. the progress: a 2pt `player.progress` line along the bottom inside edge, clipped by the radius, linear in real time (the allowed exception)
- **Never glows.** It's the only control for what's playing.

State table:

| State | Look | Motion | Haptic |
|---|---|---|---|
| appearing (first play) | rises 12pt + fades in | `curve.standard` 280 | — |
| paused | play | — | — |
| playing | pause; the line advances | `playMorph` 180 | — |
| body pressed | 0.98; shadows tighten toward contact | `.rPress` | none |
| play pressed | 0.94; well `surface.washPressed` | `.rPress` | none (non-lit; plan §9 #17) |
| buffering | 2pt ring turning in the well; time holds | `spin` | — |
| failed | subline "couldn't play · tap to retry" `status.dangerText`; play → `retry` | blur-in 220 | `error` |
| offline (not downloaded) | failed with "offline" | — | — |
| dark | raised dark 92%, inner white 8%, shadows black 45% | — | — |

Interaction table:

| Touch | Press | Haptic | Motion | Outcome | Reduce Motion |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| body | 0.98 | `sheetSnap` on settle | matched geometry (artwork, title, play) into C23b on `sheet` | the full player | 140 fade |
| play/pause | 0.94 | none | morph 180 | toggles | glyph cross-fade |
| swipe left / right | content shifts 4pt and back | `skip` | `snappy` | −15 / +30 | no shift |
| swipe down | follows, then hides | `select` | `sheetDismiss` | hidden (stays reachable in podcasts later) | fade |

Accessibility:
- one element: "now playing, <title>, 1 minute 12 seconds in. double tap to open", with the custom actions play/pause, skip back 15, skip forward 30, hide
- AX3: 80 tall, the subline hides (it stays in the label), the title gets 2 lines
- Reduce Motion: fades only, no swipe shift
- Reduce Transparency: an opaque body
- Increase Contrast: a 1pt `border.control` hairline and a 3pt progress line
- contrast: subline 5.2, play icon 5.8

Acceptance:
- Matches part 45's states and `22TR-0`'s size.
- It never overlaps the tab bar.
- The content inset grows by 64 + 12 while it shows.

Tests:
- (snapshot) paused, playing, buffering, failed and dark, plus ax3 at 80 tall
- (unit) the progress line width equals the playback fraction
- (UI) tapping the mini-player opens the full player
- (UI) the VoiceOver actions include play and skip

Proof in the PR: standard proof, plus the open-into-player recording (with C23b once merged; until then, the sheet opens with a cross-fade and the morph is added in P10).

Watch out:
