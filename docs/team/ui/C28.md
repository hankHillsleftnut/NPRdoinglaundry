### C28 · Guess card                                                  size: M
Place in the tree: journal › JR-1 › thread › guess card (later PC-2 arriving, PC-3 asked, YO-4; goals use the grey variant)
Repo · branch: NPRdoinglaundry · ui/C28-guess-card
Needs: #45 (`RPill`), #46 (`RTag` guess), UI1.0, T01        Unblocks: P09 (and C29's grey variant later)

Why: when Retro thinks it has noticed something, it asks rather than tells. It shows where the thought came from, how sure it is, and lets you answer in one tap.

Inputs:
- Spec: `design-system/components/guess-card.md`; reference §C28; context.md §3 (guesses never look like facts; "asks rather than asserts")
- Paper: legacy "c6 · conversation, you and system" `25P-0` (the guess card drawing); part 08 states `C3A-0` (a guess inside an exchange); GL-1's `c:goal-card/guess` `1G35-0` (the grey variant, for the comparison only)
- Contract: 02 §4 (`Guess`; `POST /v2/guesses/:id` → `confirmed | declined`, idempotent; `speaking` false → no cards)
- Code anchors: `RTag` guess (#46: softer dash + 4pt `inference.marker` dot if approved), `RPill`, `RSpinner`

Spec:
- **Anatomy:**
  - card `radius.card` 20, padding 16, `inference.surface`, a 1pt dashed `inference.border` (`strokeBorder(style: StrokeStyle(lineWidth: 1, dash: [4, 3]))` on `RShape.rect(20)`), wash level, no shadow
  - the "a guess" tag (`RTag.guess`) top-left
  - the guess itself, always a question, 17/24 `text.primary`
  - the why line: the `guess` glyph + "from 9 entries · steps" (Geist 12 `text.label`, per the labels rule) + the sureness word ("fairly sure"); tapping it opens why (a sheet with C32 rows: `GET /v2/guesses/:id/why`; that sheet is built in P09's last step)
  - actions: two wash pills, 36 in a 44 hit area, "that's right" (`check`) and "not quite" (`correct`)
- **"Not quite"** declines (`verdict: "no"`), then opens the composer with "actually…" so you can say it in your own words (the composer focus is P09's).

State table:

| State | Look | Motion | Haptic |
|---|---|---|---|
| rest (offered) | as above | arrives with blur-in (`.rBlurReplace`) | — |
| pill pressed | 0.94 | `.rPress` | none |
| pending | the chosen pill shows a turning ring; the other pill stays | `spin` | — |
| confirmed | the dash becomes a solid 0.5 hairline; it gains card-tier shadows; the tag fades out; "kept" | 320ms, settles onto the page (`RMotion.settle`) | `confirm` |
| corrected (declined) | the card dims to 55%; the composer opens with "actually…" | 200ms | `select` |
| answered | the pills disappear (never greyed) | fade 140 | — |
| error | "couldn't save that · try again" `status.dangerText` | blur-in | `error` |
| offline | the answer queues; "saved on this phone" (Geist 12 `text.label`) | — | `confirm` (it's kept) |
| dark | `inference.surface` dark; dashes 3 : 1 | — | — |

Interaction table:

| Touch | Press | Haptic | Motion | Outcome | Reduce Motion |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| that's right | 0.94 | `confirm` on success | pending → confirm settle | confirmed | no settle; 140 cross-fade to solid |
| not quite | 0.94 | `select` | dim 200 | declined; composer opens with "actually…" | no dim motion |
| the why line | dims 0.7 | none | sheet opens (C19.1) | why: what it rests on | sheet cross-fade |
| retry after an error | 0.94 | none | `.rPress` | resends the same verdict (idempotent) | same |

Accessibility:
- one container: "a guess: <question>. fairly sure. from 9 entries and steps." with the actions "that's right" and "not quite" and a "why" action
- AX3: the pills stack vertically
- Increase Contrast: the dash goes to 1.5pt and the sureness word to `text.primary`
- Reduce Motion: confirm without the settle
- contrast: dashed border 3.1, why line 5.2
- never colour alone: the tag and the dash carry it, not the tint

Acceptance:
- It looks unfinished (dashed, tagged) until confirmed, and never looks like a fact.
- It matches `25P-0`'s drawing; differences against the inference tokens are listed.
- No card renders when `speaking` is false.

Tests:
- (snapshot) offered, pending, confirmed, declined, error and offline, light, dark and ax3
- (snapshot) at AX3 the pills stack
- (unit) answering twice sends one verdict (idempotent on the view's side: pills disappear on the first tap)
- (UI) choosing not quite opens the composer with "actually…"
- (UI) VoiceOver offers that's right and not quite as actions

Proof in the PR: standard proof, plus the confirm-settle recording.

Watch out:
- Founder question 1 decides **where** the card sits in the v1 thread. The component doesn't care; P09 places it.
- The goals' grey variant is not this byte. Don't parametrise colours for it yet.
- Never guess about health, sexuality, religion or money: that's the brain's rule, but don't build any UI that implies those categories exist.
