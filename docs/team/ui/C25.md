### C25 · Scrubber                                                    size: M
Place in the tree: journal › JR-2 › player › scrubber (also PC-2, ON-11)
Repo · branch: NPRdoinglaundry · ui/C25-scrubber
Needs: UI3.0, T01        Unblocks: C23b

Why: you can see where you are in the brief, part by part. You can hold and slide to any part, feeling a tick as you cross into each one, and the audio jumps only when you let go.

Inputs:
- Spec: `design-system/components/scrubber.md`, **superseded where the build notes differ** (pill segments, one-line bubble); reference §C25; scrubber spacing fix c-ktepmd
- Paper: part 09 `A38-0` (versions `A3D-0`: ready, playing, held scrub); master `B1C-0` / `B99-0`; JR-2 instance `c:scrubber/chapters` `1FNE-0`
- Code anchors: `RMotion.snappy`, `.popover`; `RHaptic.chapterTick`; mono tabular text style in `RType`

Spec:
- **Hit area:** 44 tall over the whole line.
- **Track:** one capsule per part (brief) or chapter (episode), with 4pt gaps; height `size.player.scrubber` 4; fill `surface.track` (sunken); played `player.progress` inside each capsule, clipped by it; buffered `player.buffered` (hidden for briefs).
- **Thumb:**
  - 12 (`size.player.thumb`), white, raised (0.5 white inner edge, 0.5 warm hairline, contact `0 1 1.5`, cast `0 4 10 −3`)
  - its x always comes from the playback fraction (c-zgzgcy); the track is vertically centred on it
- **Below:**
  - elapsed: mono 13 `text.label`, tabular
  - the part name: `text.tertiary`
  - remaining with a true minus ("−07:18"); no total
- **Held:**
  - a long press and drag grows the thumb 12 → 20 on `snappy` and lifts it (cast `0 4 10` 16%)
  - a raised 32 capsule bubble rises over it with the part name only (Geist 500 14), clamped to the sheet's margins
  - the times follow the finger, and the row's own part label fades out
  - crossing into a new part fires `chapterTick`, and the bubble's name rolls 6pt (160ms)
  - the thumb snaps to a part start within 12pt
  - the audio seeks **on release** only
  - dragging off vertically keeps scrubbing, finer further away (the iOS rule: rate halves past 50pt, quarters past 100pt)
- A brief under a minute has no part gaps (one capsule).

State table:

| State | Look | Motion | Haptic |
|---|---|---|---|
| rest | as above | none | none |
| held | thumb 20 + bubble | `scrubPreview` 120; thumb on `snappy` | none |
| crossing a part | the name rolls | 160ms | `chapterTick` |
| release | the bubble falls and fades; the thumb settles; seek | `RMotion.popover` | none |
| buffering | buffered edge pulses (episodes); the thumb can't pass it | 900 | none |
| loading | track only, no played and no thumb | — | — |
| still generating (episodes) | track dashed past the buffered edge | — | — |
| dark | dark track, played 4.1 : 1, white thumb with a black 45% cast | — | — |

Interaction table:

| Touch | Press | Haptic | Motion | Outcome | Reduce Motion |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| tap on the track | — | none | the thumb moves on `snappy` | seek there | jump |
| long press + drag | thumb grows | `chapterTick` per part | `snappy`, `scrubPreview` | preview; seek on release | the bubble appears without rising; no roll |
| VoiceOver swipe up/down | — | none | — | ±15s | same |

Accessibility:
- adjustable: "4 minutes 12 seconds of 11 minutes 30, part 2 of 3"
- AX3: the times grow and stack (elapsed/remaining row, then the part name)
- Increase Contrast: the track one step darker and the gaps 3pt

Acceptance:
- Matches `1FNE-0` and part 09's held state.
- Seeks only on release.
- Exactly one tick per part crossing, including fast drags (crossing two parts fires two).

Tests:
- (unit) a fraction maps to the thumb x across parts with gaps
- (unit) a finger x maps back to the time, skipping the gaps
- (unit) snapping within 12pt of a part start
- (unit) a vertical offset of 60pt halves the scrub rate
- (unit) crossing two parts in one move yields two ticks
- (snapshot) rest, held, loading and dark, ax3 included
- (UI) VoiceOver increment moves forward 15 seconds

Proof in the PR: standard proof, plus a held-scrub recording with ticks marked.

Watch out:
- Never seek while dragging. It thrashes the live player (plumbing's AVPlayer).
- Gaps aren't time: the maths must exclude them, or the thumb drifts at part boundaries.
