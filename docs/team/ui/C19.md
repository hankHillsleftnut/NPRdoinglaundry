Spec: **C19.1 `RSheetHost`**, a custom presentation, decided in plan §9 #20
- **One progress value** `p ∈ [0, 1]` drives the sheet's offset, the page behind (scale 1 → `scale.behindSheet` 0.94, corners 0 → `radius.behindSheet` 12, `RShape.rect`) and the scrim (`background.scrim`, 0 → 40%; dark 60%). Nothing animates on its own clock.
- **Sheet:**
  - top corners `radius.sheet` 38 (continuous)
  - fill `background.raised`, never canvas
  - 1pt `border.highlight` top edge and a `0 −1 0` black 20% hairline
  - a grabber 36 × 5 in `border.strong`, 10pt below the top
  - max width `layout.sheetMaxWidth` 560
  - a 10pt strip of the page shows above at the full detent
- **Detents:** `.medium` (`layout.sheetDetent.medium` 0.5) and `.full`, chosen per use.
- **Content:** a flexible middle (`minHeight: 0`) so the bottom controls stay on screen on SE; the content scrolls and the controls don't; `.scrollClipDisabled()` so shadows aren't clipped.
- **Gestures:**
  - drag follows the finger 1:1
  - past the top it rubber-bands (`gesture.rubberBand` 0.55)
  - release past 140pt or faster than 800pt/s (`gesture.dismissDistance/Velocity`) dismisses on `sheetDismiss`, carrying velocity
  - otherwise it snaps to the nearest detent on `sheet`, with `sheetSnap` at rest
  - the drag hands off to the content's scroll view only when the content is at its top (the same rule as the system sheet)
- **Keyboard:** the sheet rises with the keyboard and never covers the focused field.
- **API:** `.rSheet(isPresented:, detents:, content:)` on the presenting page, plus `RSheetHost` installed once at the root of each tab (the integrator's shell: request a one-line install).
- **Rules:** never two sheets stacked (presenting a second replaces the first after the first dismisses); a sheet never opens empty (its content shows its own loading).

- two stacked buttons: the confirming one (danger recipe, the only place a destructive glow appears) above "cancel" (secondary), 4 apart

| closing | carries velocity | `RMotion.sheetDismiss` (0.32/0.90) | — |

| error | dialog stays; one line `status.dangerText` | blur-in | `error` |
| offline | confirm disabled, "needs a connection" (Geist 12 `text.label`) | — | — |
| dark | raised dark; scrim black 60% | — | — |

Interaction table:

| Touch | Press | Haptic | Motion | Outcome | Reduce Motion |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| drag the grabber or content at top | — | `sheetSnap` on settle | 1:1, then `sheet` / `sheetDismiss` | snap or dismiss | drag still works; release cross-fades 140ms |

Accessibility:
- the sheet is `.isModal` with focus contained; the title is read first; the escape action dismisses
- the dialog is announced like an alert; for destructive choices, cancel is the default action
- AX3: the sheet opens at full height and the dialog content scrolls
- Reduce Motion: 140ms cross-fade, no scale behind
- Reduce Transparency: scrim solid 60%
- Increase Contrast: the sheet's top edge is 1pt `border.control`

Steps:

Acceptance:
- Sheet, behind and scrim never disagree during a drag (frame-by-frame).
- A flick at 800pt/s from any height dismisses.
- A slow drag under 140pt snaps back.
- A new touch during a snap catches the sheet.
- The dialog matches `E25-0`'s dialog and the destructive glow appears only there.

Proof in the PR: standard proof, plus drag, flick, interrupt and keyboard recordings (C19.1), and a dialog screenshot next to `E25-0` (C19.2).

Watch out:
- The scroll and drag hand-off is where custom sheets usually feel wrong. Use one gesture recogniser on the sheet that defers to the scroll view unless its content offset is at the top (a `UIGestureRecognizerRepresentable`, iOS 18).
- Never `.animation(_:value:)` on the progress: drive it with `withAnimation(RMotion.sheet)` from the gesture's end, passing velocity.
- `size.dialog` 300 is stale. Ask the integrator to correct the token to 342, or use the margin rule.
