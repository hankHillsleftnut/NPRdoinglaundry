### C20 · Menu (popover host, rows; message long press)                size: M
Place in the tree: journal › JR-1 › message › long-press menu (later: PC-1 title menu, player "…", settings value rows)

Needs: C19.1 (the overlay host), T01        Unblocks: P09 (long press on your message), C23b's "…" (later), ST-2/ST-7

Why: choices grow out of the thing you touched, quickly and calmly, and close faster than they opened. A long press on what you sent gives you the obvious things to do with it.

Inputs:
- Spec: `design-system/components/title-menu.md` (measured from the founder's recording); reference §C20; message.md (long press)
- Paper: legacy "c4 · title menu, paywall, settings" `102-0` (the filmstrip: say so in the PR); conversation master `B1B-0` and part 08 states `C3A-0` (long press)
- Code anchors: `RMotion.popover`, `.snappy`, `RMotion.exit`; `RHaptic.select`, `.longPress`

Spec:
- **`RMenu`** (the panel):
  - anchored to its source's frame (from an anchor preference); grows toward bottom-trailing, or toward leading near the trailing edge
  - width hugs the longest row, min 196
  - radius `radius.popover` 22; `background.raised`; 0.5 warm hairline; `elevation.popover` (cast `0 18 40 −10` + contact `0 2 6`); 4pt inner padding
  - rows `size.popoverRow` 50, text 15
  - a selected row sits on a `surface.wash` pill, radius 18 (concentric), with a thin 18pt ink check (`ink.default`, no disc) trailing
  - disabled rows are `text.tertiary` with a reason
  - a veil `background.veil` 6% over the page
- **Choreography** (measured; build these exact numbers):
  - 0–66ms: the source dims to `opacity.sourcePressed` 0.7 (`duration.sourceDim`); nothing else moves
  - at 66ms: the menu appears at `scale.popoverFrom` 0.86, `blur.enter` 8, opacity 0
  - 66–230ms: it grows on `RMotion.popover`, sharpening; the veil fades in; at settle, `select`
  - choosing: the pill slides on `snappy`, the check cross-fades, and the menu closes after 120ms
  - closing: reverse and faster, `duration.popoverExit` 110 on `curve.exit`, shrinking into the source with blur
- **Gestures:**
  - tap outside, swipe up or re-tap the source to close
  - long press, slide and release selects in one gesture (rows highlight under the finger with `select` on each change)
- **The message long-press variant** (built in this byte, used by P09):
  - the pressed bubble lifts (raised card tier: `depth.shadow.cast`, scale 1.02 on `popover`); the `longPress` haptic fires at the 0.4s threshold
  - v1 rows follow founder question 2 (lean: "copy" and "open this entry ›"); the row list is data, so v2 adds "correct this" and "why you said this" without a redesign

State table:

| State | Look | Motion | Haptic |
|---|---|---|---|
| source pressed | 0.7 | `sourceDim` 66ms | — |
| open | menu + veil | choreography | `select` on settle |

| closing | shrink into the source | 110ms `curve.exit` | — |
| dark | raised dark, hairline white 8%, cast black 45%, veil black 20% | — | — |

Interaction table:

| Touch | Press | Haptic | Motion | Outcome | Reduce Motion |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| tap the source | 0.7 dim | `select` on settle | choreography | open | fade in place 140ms, full size, no blur |
| long press your bubble 0.4s | lift | `longPress` | `popover` | open the message menu | lift without scale; fade 140 |
| tap a row | pill moves | `select` | `snappy` + close 110 | the row's action | instant pill; fade out 140 |
| slide and release | highlight follows | `select` per row | `snappy` | selects on release | same without the slide motion |
| tap outside / swipe up | — | none | close 110 | closes, nothing chosen | fade 140 |

Accessibility:
- the source is "<title>, showing <current>, menu"; for the message it's a custom action "more" on the bubble (VoiceOver doesn't need the long press)
- rows are a list, the selected one carries `.isSelected`; the menu is `.isModal`; escape closes
- AX3: rows ≥ 50 grow, and the menu widens to screen − margins, then wraps
- Increase Contrast: a 1pt `border.control` ring
- Reduce Transparency: veil 10% solid, no blur

Acceptance:
- The timings match the measured choreography within one frame at 60fps on the recording.
- Close is faster than open.
- It never opens from the screen centre.
- A tab change while open closes it without choosing.

Tests:
- (unit) the anchor maths grows toward leading when the source is within 196pt of the trailing edge
- (snapshot) open with a selected row and a disabled row, light, dark and ax3
- (snapshot) the lifted bubble with its menu
- (UI) long-pressing your message shows copy and open this entry
- (UI) the VoiceOver "more" action on a bubble opens the same menu
- (UI) tapping outside closes without choosing

Proof in the PR: standard proof, plus a frame-stepped recording of open, select and close next to the measured timeline.

Watch out:
- SwiftUI's `Menu` and `.contextMenu` can't take these values. This is a custom overlay on C19.1's host, so it can never sit under a sheet.
- `select` fires on settle, not on touch-down.
- Copy uses `UIPasteboard` and writes text only. No logging of what was copied.
