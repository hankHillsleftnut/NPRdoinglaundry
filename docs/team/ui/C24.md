### C24 · Transport                                                   size: M

Repo · branch: NPRdoinglaundry · ui/C24-transport
Needs: UI3.0, T01        Unblocks: C23b

Why: play, pause and skip are where your thumb expects them. The play button morphs rather than swaps, and every tap has a feel.

Inputs:
- Spec: `design-system/components/transport.md`; reference §C24 (the Paper master's recess recipe wins over the spec's older one)
- Paper:
  - part 09 `A38-0`; master `B1C-0`
  - JR-2 instances `c:transport/recess/playing` `1FMV-0`, `c:button/raised/pause` `1FMY-0`
  - glyphs `c:icon/skip-back-15` `1FN1-0`, `c:icon/skip-forward-30` `1FMW-0`, `c:icon/pause` `1FMZ-0`
- Code anchors: `RMotion.snappy`; `RHaptic.press`, `.skip` (if missing from tokens, ask the integrator: the reference lists `skip`); `RSpinner`

Spec:
- **Tray:** a capsule 64 high, radius 32 (`RShape.pill`), recessed in `surface.washPressed`, drawn with inner shadows on the canvas colour (a deep top shade, a light catch on the bottom lip), not a lighter fill.
- **Skips:** the shipped `skip-back-15` and `skip-forward-30` template glyphs (the numerals are part of the path), 28pt, `text.secondary`, in 44 frames.
- **Play well:** 48, raised (`background.raised`, double stroke, warm contact + cast), with the play/pause glyph `text.primary` centred by its own frame (no manual offset). **Never glows.**
- **Outside the tray:** "…" (`more`, 44) on the left; speed (mono 15 tabular `text.secondary`) on the right. Episode mode only; for briefs they're hidden unless Paper `1FMV-0` draws them (check, and list the decision).

State table:

| State | Look | Motion | Haptic |
|---|---|---|---|
| paused | play | — | — |
| playing | pause | `playMorph` 180 (a custom glyph morph: scale 0.8 + opacity cross, since the glyphs aren't SF Symbols) | — |
| play pressed | 0.94; cast collapses to contact | `.rPress` | `press` |
| skip pressed | the arrow turns 20° in its direction and back; the time rolls | `snappy` | `skip` |
| buffering | a 2pt `text.label` ring turns around the glyph; the time holds | `spin` 900 | — |
| ended | play → `retry` ("play again"); forward disabled | morph | — |
| failed | the well shows `retry`; one line above: "couldn't load this brief" | blur-in 220 | `error` |
| offline (not downloaded) | skips disabled + "offline · it'll play when you're back online" | — | — |
| disabled control | `opacity.disabled` 0.4 (`.compositingGroup()` first) | — | — |
| dark | carved tray (dark canvas + deep inner shade), well raised dark | — | — |

Interaction table:

| Touch | Press | Haptic | Motion | Outcome | Reduce Motion |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| play/pause | 0.94 | `press` | morph 180 | toggles | 140 cross-fade of the glyph |
| skip back | turn −20° | `skip` | `snappy` | −15s | no turn |
| skip forward | turn +20° | `skip` | `snappy` | +30s (+15 under a minute) | no turn |
| retry | 0.94 | `press` | `.rPress` | reloads | same |

Accessibility:
- "skip back 15 seconds", "play" / "pause", "skip forward 30 seconds"
- the media keys and the lock screen are plumbing's (Now Playing); these are in-app only
- AX3: the controls keep their size
- Increase Contrast: the tray gets 1pt `border.control` and the skip icons `text.primary`
- every control ≥ 44

Acceptance:
- Matches `1FMV-0` in light and dark.
- The glyphs are optically centred without offsets.
- Rapid taps queue no extra skips beyond one per tap.

Tests:
- (snapshot) paused, playing, buffering, ended, failed, offline and dark, ax3 included
- (unit) skip forward under one minute is 15 seconds
- (UI) play then pause shows the right labels to VoiceOver

Proof in the PR: standard proof, plus a morph and skip recording.

Watch out:
- The skip glyph SVG carries a raw `#5F5D5B`. Ship it as a template image tinted by token.
- The play never glows: this is the founder's rule for the transport, and different from the brief card.
