### C17 · Top bar (journal, settings, entry)                           size: M
Place in the tree: journal › JR-1 › top bar (also settings › every page, journal › JR-3/JR-4)

Why: every screen tells you where you are and how to get back, in one consistent bar. On the journal it gets out of your way as soon as you start writing or scrolling.

Spec:
- **Variants** (`RTopBar.Variant`):
  1. `.journal(title: "journal", collapsed: Bool)`:
     - leading: the drawer button, `RIconButton` size 36 in a 44 hit area, glyph `drawer`, label "all journals"
     - at rest: inky large title after it, `type.display.title` (Exposure 34, `tracking.title`; exact values from `get_computed_styles` on `1FM0-0`)
     - collapsed: a small centred name in Geist (take size and weight from the collapsed state in `B2I-0`); the drawer button never moves
  2. `.settings(title:)`: back `RIconButton` 36 wash in 44 · centred title Geist 14 medium `text.secondary` · a 44 spacer. ST-1 also has back.
  3. `.entry(trailing: .edit | .editing(onCancel:, onSave:))`: back · trailing "edit" (text button). Editing shows "cancel · edit this entry · save", with save in plain ink. No title at rest.
  4. `.label(title:, trailing:)`: the JR-3 drawer's label bar, read from `1FPD-0`.
  5. Onboarding, the other tab titles and the sheet header are built with their first page (P02, P13/P16/P18, C23b).
- **Layout:** a 44 bar under the status bar, with 16 side insets (`spacing.4`) and the button keyline per `9N0-0`'s checks. The bar has no background of its own: on the journal the page's top edge is `.journalTop` blur (UI0.1); on settings and entry pages it's plain canvas.
- **Collapse driver:** the page passes `collapsed`. The page computes it from `onScrollGeometryChange` (offset > 8pt past the top) OR composer focus. The bar never reads the scroll view itself.

State table:

| State | Look | Motion | Haptic |
|---|---|---|---|
| rest (journal) | drawer button + inky large title | none | none |
| collapsed | small centred Geist name; large title gone | title shrinks and moves into place: a matched-geometry move on `RMotion.snappy`; reverses at the top with the keyboard down | none |
| button pressed | button `scale.pressSmall` 0.94 | `.rPress` (80 in / 240 out) | none (non-lit) |
| editing (entry) | cancel · "edit this entry" · save | `.rBlurReplace` 200ms on the trailing group | none |
| save disabled (no change) | save `text.tertiary` | `curve.standard` 140 | none |
| dark | canvas dark; text tokens invert | — | — |

Interaction table:

| Touch | Press | Haptic | Motion | Outcome | Reduce Motion |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| drawer button | 0.94 | none | `.rPress` | opens the drawer (UI1.1) | same |
| back | 0.94 | none | `.rPress` | pops (system push transition) | system cross-fade |
| edit | text dims to `opacity.pressed` | none | 80/240 | entry enters editing | same |
| save | ink dims | `confirm` when the save lands | — | saves, exits editing | same |
| scroll past 8pt or focus the composer | — | none | collapse on `snappy` | collapsed | 140ms cross-fade, no move |

Accessibility:
- the title has the `.isHeader` trait
- buttons are labelled "all journals", "back", "edit", "cancel", "save"
- the collapsed name keeps the header trait (one header, never two)
- Dynamic Type: the large title scales to AX5 and wraps to two lines before it truncates; at AX3 and above it collapses earlier (offset > 0)
- the button hit area stays 44 at every size
- Increase Contrast: the settings title becomes `text.primary`

Steps: journal variant (rest, collapsed) → settings → entry/editing → label → gallery and snapshots → collapse recording.

Acceptance:
- Every variant and state above matches its Paper node in light, dark and AX3.
- Collapse and expand can be interrupted: scrolling back mid-move retargets without a jump.
- The title never overlaps the drawer button at any width (SE included).
- No title on the entry variant.

Proof in the PR: standard proof, plus the collapse recording on JR-1's gallery (scroll down, back up, interrupted) and an SE screenshot.

Watch out:
- The matched geometry needs one `@Namespace` owned by the page, passed in. Under Reduce Motion, don't run the matched move at all, or the text stretches.
- The collapse driver must be a `Bool`, not the raw offset, or the page re-renders every frame.
- Paper's SF text runs wider than iOS's. Don't add tracking to match it.
