### C23b · Player sheet, brief mode                                  size: L (split if it runs over: C23b.1 frame + header + transcript, C23b.2 now/next/played/quiet)
Place in the tree: journal › JR-2 › player sheet (brief mode); episode mode is C23e with PC-2 later

Why: today's brief opens into a calm listening space. You see what's playing now and why it's there for you, what's next, and the words as they're spoken, with the controls always under your thumb.

Spec:
- **Frame (C23b.1):**
  - a C19.1 sheet at the full detent, `background.raised`, no blue gradient
  - header row: the mark 22, "today's brief" (Geist 500 14), and a `today` tag on the left; share and close (34 wash circles in 44, 17pt glyphs, stroke 2) on the right
  - the middle region flexes (`minHeight: 0`)
  - the scrubber and transport are pinned above the home indicator on every device: scrubber top padding `spacing-8`, transport top `spacing-3`
- **Transcript column:**
  - Exposure `displayInky` weight 399 (`inkCard`), `text.xl` (take the size from `1FMR-0`'s computed styles; the tokens say 22, the build notes 20: list it), `tracking.card`, leading 1.3, 20 between lines, width `layout.readingWidth` 326
  - the spoken part `text.primary`, every other part `text.tertiary`; the "line" is a brief **part** (the contract gives part timing, not line timing)
  - the column glides to keep the spoken part at the same height (`transcriptFollow` 420, `curve.standard`): colours cross-fade first (90ms), then the column glides
  - scrolling it yourself pauses following and shows a "back to now" pill above the scrubber; following resumes after `followResume` 4s idle
  - tapping a part jumps to it
  - blur bands `.transcriptTop` 96 (SE 64) and `.transcriptBottom` 140 (SE 80); no top blur at the very start
- **Brief items (C23b.2):**
  - the "now" card labelled "playing now", with the why line "here for your goal: <goal>" (from `part.why`) and the source line "from …" ›
  - "next" rows
  - played: "played · 3 of 3"
  - quiet day: one inky line, a one-part 40-second scrubber, no correct pill
  - "correct this part" is a wash pill, leading-aligned between the transcript and the scrubber; it hides while scrubbing; it opens the correction flow for the part being spoken (UI1.2's sheet, `about` = the part's first source)
- **Two layout states:** ready shows the big inky headline above the transcript; on play, the title scrolls up and the header-left swaps to the headline in Geist 500 14, one line (as a scroll, not a cross-fade; under Reduce Motion, a cut).
- **Choreography (opening):**
  1. touch-down 0.98 on the source (no haptic)
  2. the sheet rises on `sheet`; the title morphs from the source (matched geometry: the brief card's headline or the mini-player's title)
  3. the page behind scales to 0.94, rounds to 12 and gets a 40% scrim, all on one progress value
  4. the transcript fades up 8pt with a 60ms stagger after the title; the controls arrive last
  5. `sheetSnap` on settle
- **Dragging down:** only from the header, grabber or title; the transcript scrolls without fighting the sheet.

Accessibility:
- VoiceOver order: close → title → why → transcript (one text block; the current part is announced on change only if asked) → scrubber → transport → pills
- AX3: the title and transcript grow, the transcript shows two parts, the controls stay fixed
- Reduce Motion: 140ms cross-fade, title cross-fade instead of morph, the transcript jumps
- Reduce Transparency: solid 60% scrim, opaque bands
- Increase Contrast: other parts at 70%; tray and pills get 1pt `border.control`

Acceptance:
- Matches `1FMR-0`, `1FO0-0`, `1FNV-0` and part 10's played and quiet states.
- On SE the spoken part is always in the clear middle and the controls never leave the screen.
- Following never runs while you're touching the column.
