✓ purpose
one shape, three heights: neutral offers a light choice (chapters, not right), raised is the real secondary action (add, connect, retry), lit is the one next step
✓ colour
neutral surface.wash; raised surface.secondaryAction (cream), never blue; lit glow.primary, the only blue (surfaces "where blue goes")
✓ type
Geist label (font.label), not mono (screen rule 4); 14 medium on neutral, 14 semibold on raised (rule 15), 16 semibold on lit; leading.tight, tracking.label; lowercase verbs and nouns
✓ space
32 tall; icon side 8 so the 16 glyph sits on the cap's centre, label side 12, 4 between; lit 48 with 16 / 20
✓ shape
true pills: radius.control (height / 2); no inner wells, so nothing to make concentric
✓ depth
one level each: wash 0.5 flat; raised 2 = inner white stroke brighter on top, 0.5 warm hairline, contact 0 1 1.5 + cast 0 4 6 −2; lit 5 = rim, bloom, lower edge, 2pt ring, halo, sky cast
— texture
under 44pt tall: no grain (lit adds grain only at 60, the hero button)
✓ icon
every glyph is a shipped inked icon at its 20pt optical size, drawn 16 in pills and 20 in the lit pill; tinted icon.default, white on lit
✓ tokens
surface.wash(.pressed), surface.secondaryAction, depth.stroke.*, depth.shadow.contact/cast, glow.primary.*, depth.tint.primary, radius.control, font.label, text.md/base
✓ optical centring
label set on line-height 1 and centred on its x-height; the asymmetric padding (8 / 12) balances the glyph's soft edge against the text's hard one
✓ contrast
text.primary on wash ≈ 16 : 1; on cream ≈ 17 : 1; white on the sky gradient ≥ 4.5 : 1 at the bottom stop (checked by contrast-pairs)
✓ hit area
44 tall around every 32 visual (dashed blue at 3×); the pill is always wider than 44; lit is 48, already over
✓ states
rest, pressed, focused drawn; neutral darkens to wash.pressed, raised sinks its shadow (stays on its level), lit takes the pressed gradient and drops the bloom; disabled only for lit, with a reason beside it
✓ motion
press scale 0.96 in 80ms, back 240ms on the spring; lit fade 200ms when focused; Reduce Motion: no scale
✓ haptic
lit: haptic.press on touch down; neutral and raised: none (they are light choices)
✓ goals
a quiet page with one lit next step (rule 5); secondary actions obvious by depth, not colour (rule 15)
✓ wireframe
matches the mid-fi's pill row under the brief and the raised "connect / add" pills in onboarding
