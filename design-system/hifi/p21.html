✓ purpose
labels name a group so you can find your place; they never act, so they never look tappable
✓ colour
text.label (paper 620) on the canvas: quieter than the content it heads, louder than the background
✓ type
Geist 500, 12 (text.sm), tracking.label +0.01em, leading.snug; lowercase. Relative days ("today", "yesterday", a weekday) up to 6 days, then "tue 14 oct". The mid-fi's serif settings titles are replaced: type.md says section labels are Geist 500
✓ space
thread: 8 above the first message, 40 between days (rule 32). drawer: 8 above, 24 between days. settings: 12 above the group, 32 between groups (space.section)
— shape
no container: grouping by space, not lines or pills (layout rule: space first)
✓ depth
page level 0: content, never raised (surfaces rule 2)
— texture
none
— icon
none: a word is clearer than a calendar glyph here
✓ tokens
font.label, text.sm, font.weight.medium, tracking.label, leading.snug, text.label, spacing 2 / 3 / 6 / 8 / 10; token-check 0
✓ optical centring
thread labels centre on the reading column (326), not the screen, so they sit over the messages they head
✓ contrast
text.label on canvas ≈ 5.1 : 1 (passes 4.5 at 12pt)
— hit area
not interactive; VoiceOver treats each as a header (accessibilityAddTraits(.isHeader)), so rotor jumps work
✓ states
rest only; sticky in the drawer (the current day pins under the search field while its rows scroll); Dynamic Type grows them to AX3, then they wrap
✓ motion
the drawer's sticky label swaps with a 120ms crossfade when the next day pushes it up; Reduce Motion: instant
— haptic
none
✓ goals
Journal is one thread grouped by day (rule 29); the drawer is its index by day; labels make both scannable
✓ wireframe
same positions as the mid-fi (JR-1 day labels, JR-3 drawer days, Settings groups); settings titles change from serif to Geist
