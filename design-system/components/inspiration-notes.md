# inspiration, translated

What the founder sent (20 Oct) and how each idea became ours, rather than being copied.

| Seen | Why it works | Ours |
|---|---|---|
| Title menu that grows from the title, dims first, overshoots a little, closes faster | The press lands before anything moves; the origin explains the menu; leaving is instant | [title-menu](./title-menu.md), with the timings measured from the recording |
| Player sheet over a stacked, dimmed screen | You can see it's a layer you can push away | [player](./player.md): stacked strip, behind-screen scale 0.94, one shared progress value |
| Player led by big show art | Their shows are other people's brands | **Not taken.** Our page leads with the title and the transcript: the content is the cover |
| Floating mini-player pill with content fading under it | Always in reach, never covering text | [mini-player](./mini-player.md), with a progress hairline and our materials |
| Transport in one capsule, play in a raised well | The thumb finds play without looking | [transport](./transport.md) |
| "Today" tag, played checkbox | Status at a glance, in place | [tag](./tag.md) |
| Locked insights with a blurred count | Shows what's there without giving it away | Paywall benefit drawings from the person's **own** data, never fake numbers ([paywall](./paywall.md)) |
| Two plan buttons with a save badge breaking the edge | Comparison at a glance; the badge reads as attached | [paywall](./paywall.md) plan pair |
| Settings rows with an "add" pill and ↗ for external links | Says what will happen before you tap | [settings-row](./settings-row.md) |
| A tilted paper card telling the app's story | A human moment inside settings | The story card: "read why retrospect exists" |
| Condensed heavy headlines | Their brand | **Not taken.** Ours stays Hedvig serif, lowercase, with restraint |
| Green accent glow | Their brand | **Not taken.** Our one sky glow, built from the paywall stack |
